Delivered behavior
Existing manual mutations now require an attributable human with fresh Telegram permissions, or a sufficiently privileged human’s approval of an immutable anonymous request. Cached administrators, factory ownership, platform administration, pseudo-users and channel senders confer no mutation authority.
Anonymous reviews expire within 120 seconds. They bind the bot, group, original/review messages, thread, target, arguments, reason, duration and required rights. Invalid attempts retain the request; valid approvals atomically claim it once and recheck authority before execution. Execution failure consumes the request. Public output omits reviewer identity; private audit records the actual approving human and distinguishes anonymous from known-human initiation.
Targets must be positive human IDs resolved through replies, entities, observed usernames or numeric IDs. Unknown, stale or ambiguous usernames, pseudo-users, bots and freshly protected administrators/creators are denied. Reasons retain their first token and internal spacing; reasons over 256 characters are rejected.
Temporary sanctions accept 60 seconds–365 days inclusive. Missing or malformed /tban and /tmute timers cause no sanction, deletion or domain write. Numeric/sign/dot-leading malformed input cannot silently become a permanent action. Reason-only /ban 20 spam remains supported. General timers still accept 10s.
Typed results distinguish primary completion from ancillary failure. A kick requires both ban and unban; failed restoration cannot announce success. Unmute uses actual chat defaults, and night closure requires a stored restorable snapshot. Bulk deletion reports requested and acknowledged IDs while keeping the confirmed deleted count unknown.
